# Privacy

How the system complies with the LGPD (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados).

## What goes here

- `inventory.md` — record of processing: each category of personal data, purpose, legal basis (LGPD art. 7 and 11), where it is stored, who can access it, retention and sharing with processors
- `data-subject-rights.md` — how each right (access, correction, deletion, portability, ...) is fulfilled, by whom and within which deadline
- `processors.md` — third parties that process personal data on our behalf, with links to their DPAs
- Data protection impact assessments, when required

## What does not go here

- The public-facing privacy policy → [terms/](../terms/README.md)
- Column-level details → [data/](../../data/README.md), which links back here

## Rules

- A change that collects new personal data or uses it for a new purpose updates the inventory in the same PR.

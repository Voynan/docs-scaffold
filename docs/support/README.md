# Support

What support needs to answer users quickly and consistently.

## What goes here

- `faq.md` — frequent questions and their answers
- `known-issues.md` — current problems users may hit, their workaround and the issue tracking the fix
- `canned-responses.md` — standard replies

## What does not go here

- Bug tracking → GitHub issues; link them instead of describing their status here
- Internal procedures → [operations/runbooks/](../operations/runbooks/README.md)

## Rules

- Remove a known issue when its fix is released.

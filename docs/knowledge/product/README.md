# Product

Why the product exists and where it is going.

## What goes here

- `vision.md` — the problem space, target customers and long-term direction
- `value-proposition.md` — jobs, pains and gains the product addresses
- `personas.md` — who uses the product and what they need
- `competitors.md` — alternatives users have today and how we differ
- `roadmap.md` — themes and outcomes by horizon (now, next, later), not a feature list with dates

## What does not go here

- Epic-level problem statements and success metrics → [prds/](../../prds/README.md)
- Plans and limits → [pricing/](../../pricing/README.md)

## Naming

Lowercase kebab-case, one topic per file.

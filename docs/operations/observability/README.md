# Observability

What we watch in production and what to do when it fires.

## What goes here

- `alerts.md` — one entry per alert: condition, severity, what it means, likely causes, and the runbook to follow
- `dashboards.md` — each dashboard: link, audience and the questions it answers
- SLOs, when defined, with their NFR IDs

## What does not go here

- Product analytics → [metrics/](../../metrics/README.md)

## Rules

- An alert without a runbook or a clear action is noise; fix it or delete it.
